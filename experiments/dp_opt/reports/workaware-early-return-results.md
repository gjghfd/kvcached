A universal diagnostic form within the measured same-sparse-graph populations is

`delta_cadence = F_m,N(max(n0,n1)) - F_m,N(ceil(N/2)) + beta_m,N * delta_peak_context`,

where`F(k)=sum_{j=ceil(N/2)+1..k} gamma_j`, all`gamma_j>=0`and`beta>=0`.

inventing a dense capture or extracting a kernel-attention fraction. Both the
linear-count alternative and this monotone alternative use the same full raw
